About FarmSmart

FarmSmart is a cutting-edge weather-based crop advisory system designed to empower farmers with timely, location-specific insights. Our mission is to transform traditional farming into smart, data-driven agriculture using technology that’s both powerful and easy to use.

Key Features

  • Real-time Weather Data: Stay up-to-date with weather conditions in your region.
  • Personalized Crop Recommendations: Get crop suggestions tailored to your location and weather.
  • User Dashboard: Track your crop history and gain insights into weather trends and crop performance.
  • Easy Access: Access all features through an intuitive, user-friendly interface.
